\section{Critical and high findings in detail}

\subsection*{F1 --- the abstention judge could never say \enquote{refused}}

\begin{description}[style=nextline]
  \item[Evidence] \code{judge_abstention} calls the model with \code{max_tokens=5} and counts a refusal only if the reply begins with the full word \code{REFUSED} \ev{src/vidyarag/evaluation/abstention.py:128-144}. Reproduced live: with 5 tokens the model's output is cut to \code{"REF"} (finish reason \code{length}) for a baseline prose refusal and even for the pipeline's own abstention sentence; with 50 tokens it returns \code{"REFUSED"}. All 275 cached verdicts were \enquote{not abstained}. No test calls the function.
  \item[Impact] Every judged abstention in the five committed runs is zero; see F2.
  \item[Fix] \code{max_tokens} of at least 16 or structured output with an enum; a tolerant prefix match; unit tests with a fake client; then re-grade the cached answers of all five profiles. Effort: an hour of code, plus a grading run and documentation updates.
\end{description}

\subsection*{F2 --- the headline abstention claim}

\begin{description}[style=nextline]
  \item[Evidence] The stored answers of \code{baseline}, \code{rerank} and \code{decompose} decline all 12 unanswerable questions in prose. Re-judged with the repository's own prompt and a 50-token limit, all three score recall 1.000 (12 of 12), as does \code{corrective}; \code{guarded} scores 0.917 \tagM.
  \item[Where the claim appears] The README results table and narrative (\enquote{Abstention went from never refusing to refusing 11--12 of 12}), \code{docs/EVALUATION.md}, the \code{corrective} profile's header, the corrective loop's docstring, the Space README, and commit messages.
  \item[Impact] The project's most prominent result does not hold. What the self-check demonstrably does is standardise abstention: one fixed, citation-free, machine-countable response.
  \item[Fix] Correct the documents after re-grading; restate the loop's contribution accurately.
\end{description}

\subsection*{F3 --- latency counted twice}

\begin{description}[style=nextline]
  \item[Evidence] The \code{corrective} stage encloses stages recorded inside it, and \code{total_ms} sums all stages \ev{src/vidyarag/pipeline.py:230-238}, \ev{src/vidyarag/observe/trace.py:128-130}. In four measured queries the excess over wall-clock time equalled the nested stages to within a millisecond (for example 37,431\,ms reported against 19,184\,ms real).
  \item[Impact] Committed means of 22,031 and 21,738\,ms, the demo's total row, the API's \code{total_ms}, and \enquote{22 s per query} in \code{docs/DESIGN.md}.
  \item[Fix] Measure the total as wall-clock time, or record child stages separately; add a test; re-run to correct the figures.
\end{description}

\subsection*{F4 --- tokens and cost missing}

\begin{description}[style=nextline]
  \item[Evidence] \code{add_usage} is called only in \code{generate/answer.py}. A measured grading call used 2,497 input and 612 output tokens, costing more at list price than the recorded generation call.
  \item[Impact] Reported tokens and cost for \code{corrective}, \code{guarded} and \code{decompose}; \$0.00027 per query in the README is plausibly two to three times too low \tagI.
  \item[Fix] Record usage in \code{grade_answer} and \code{decompose}.
\end{description}

\subsection*{F5 --- unverified answers presented as verified}

\begin{description}[style=nextline]
  \item[Evidence] A grading error makes the attempt unmeasured, and the policy accepts it \ev{src/vidyarag/correct/policy.py:68-69}. The demo prints \enquote{Self-check passed after 1 attempt(s)} whenever a corrective record exists and the answer is not an abstention \ev{app/app.py:132-139}. During this analysis a grading call failed with \code{503 UNAVAILABLE} and the answer was returned exactly as if verified; the next two grading attempts also returned 503 before one succeeded.
  \item[Impact] On a busy free tier, the self-check silently stops checking.
  \item[Fix] Count and surface unmeasured attempts: a \code{verified} flag in the API response, a distinct demo message, a log line, and an evaluation aggregate.
\end{description}

\section{What was checked and found to be right}

A findings chapter can leave the impression that nothing works. These properties were verified directly:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Citations cannot point outside the supplied passages}, including grouped markers; tested and exercised on constructed and real answers.
  \item \textbf{The corpus pipeline is reproducible}: checksums re-validated; a from-scratch rebuild produced the same 3,608 chunks.
  \item \textbf{Deterministic retrieval metrics are correct} and computed from the right lists; reranking left pool recall unchanged and improved recall@context and MRR as reported.
  \item \textbf{The decomposition result is real}: split multi-hop recall@context fell from 0.864 to 0.727.
  \item \textbf{The guard false-positive claim is true}: 0 of 3,608 passages matched.
  \item \textbf{Every number in the README results table matches a committed run file}; the problems above lie in what three of those numbers measure.
  \item \textbf{The suite passes} (401 tests) with coverage above its floor, and CI and the Docker workflow are green on the analysed commit.
  \item \textbf{The demo's three advertised behaviours work} on the live Space.
  \item \textbf{Secrets hygiene holds}: ignore rules, two layers of gitleaks, secrets passed through the platform's secret store.
  \item \textbf{The documentation is unusually candid}: it already records decomposition's failure, the quality gains that were not real, the untuned thresholds, the rarely used retry and the definitional-question weakness.
\end{itemize}

\section{Side effects of this analysis}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{No tracked file was changed}, and nothing was committed or pushed.
  \item \textbf{Local untracked files were rewritten}: \code{data/ingest_run.json} now describes a scratch rebuild (F17), and \code{data/raw/manifest.json} was regenerated by re-running \code{download}, with the same checksums and new timestamps.
  \item \textbf{Free-tier Gemini quota was used} for re-judging 262 stored answers, traced queries and re-grading.
\end{itemize}

\end{recommended}
